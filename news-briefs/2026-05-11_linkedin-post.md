One week in African film.

4️⃣ AISFM — Africa's first dedicated Short Film Market — wrapped its inaugural edition.
Gbenga Adeoti and Rwandan co-founders Kagabo Nkubiri and Theodore Ishimwe just built the missing rung: distribution infrastructure for shorts.

5️⃣ Next week: Nomadic Film Space at Cannes (14–15 May).
Afreximbank, Docubox, Institut Français, SACD, CNC. The $1B Africa Film Fund moves from announcement to investment strategy.

Policy. Talent. Infrastructure. All three lit up in one week.
