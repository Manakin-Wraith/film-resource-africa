![](https://techcentral.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/showmax-1500-800.jpg)

## 🏛️ Policy & Regulation

### Canada–South Africa Audiovisual Co-Production Treaty (in force since 1 Jan 2026)
![](https://culturecustodian.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/South-Africa-PR-1.jpg)
- **What:** Modernized treaty replaces the 1997 agreement. Approved co-pros qualify as national productions in both countries — eligible for Telefilm Canada, provincial film commissions, federal Canadian tax credits AND South Africa's Film & TV Production Incentive (up to 35–40% QSAPE rebate).
- **Who:** Telefilm Canada + Government of South Africa
- **Where:** Bilateral — Canada / South Africa
- **When:** Treaty signed Cape Town September 2024; entered into force 1 January 2026
- **Link:** https://culturecustodian.com/canada-and-south-africa-launch-new-film-co-production-treaty/
- **Image:** https://culturecustodian.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/South-Africa-PR-1.jpg

**FRA angle:** This is exactly the kind of pathway PRS v1 should make legible. SA writers/producers with a Canadian co-pro partner now have a new stack: Telefilm + CMF + SODEC + South African DTIC rebate. Strong candidate for the "International Co-pro — Canada" pathway plugin under consideration for PRS Phase 1 (per CLAUDE.md pending decisions).
