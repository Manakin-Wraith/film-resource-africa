# FVG Film Fund — Second Call 2026

## TL;DR

Italian regional production fund. **Non-European production companies can apply directly** as sole, co-, or executive producer. Window 1 May – **30 June 2026**. Up to **€350k for fiction**, **€40k for documentary/short**. Catch: funded percentage is of FVG-regional spend, so the project must place a real component (shoot, post, scoring, animation pipeline) in Friuli Venezia Giulia, Italy.

This is one of the cleanest non-EU-friendly European regional funds I've seen — most Italian regional funds are EU-only by structure. Worth a real shortlist exercise across the FRA member base before 30 June.

---

## Verified facts (primary source: fvgfilmcommission.com/en/film-fund, fetched 2026-05-26)

**Call window:** 1 May 2026 → 30 June 2026, 23:59 (postmark date is proof — physical postal submission to Trieste).

**Three call cycles per year:** 28 Feb / 30 June / 30 Sep. This is the **second** of 2026.

**Application precondition:** application must be submitted *before the start of filming*. Projects already in production are ineligible.

**Eligible applicants:**
- Italian audiovisual production companies (ATECO 59.11 / 59.12)
- EU production companies
- **Non-EU production companies** (equivalent NACE Rev. 2 codes)
- As sole producer, co-producer, or executive producer

**Eligible work types:**
- Feature fiction (≥52 min)
- Fiction TV series
- Documentary
- Documentary series
- Short film

**Funding amounts (corrected against LinkedIn-post claims):**

*Fiction or series:*
| Score | Intensity | Cap |
|---|---|---|
| ≥ 90 pts | 30% of FVG cost estimate | **€350,000** |
| 81–89 pts | 25% | €250,000 |
| 75–80 pts | 20% | €150,000 |

*Documentary or short film:*
| Score | Intensity | Cap |
|---|---|---|
| ≥ 86 pts | 40% of FVG cost estimate | **€40,000** |
| 75–85 pts | 25% | €20,000 |

**Cashflow:** up to 50% advance on bank or insurance surety. Grants **≤ €15,000 require no surety**. Balance within 90 days of report receipt.

**Evaluation:** 30 days after deadline (Technical Committee).

**Scoring matrix (fiction/series, total 100):**
- Promotion of FVG territory (8) · Originality (10) · Use of local crew (15) · Distribution prospects (10) · Credentials of producer/director/cast (10) · **Direct economic impact in FVG (18)** · "FVG effect" — duration of filming in FVG (5) · Production facility (15) · Image of FVG (5) · Green protocol (2) · FVG Audiovisual Fund contribution (2)

**Scoring matrix (doc/short, total 100):**
- Promotion of FVG territory (15) · Use of local crew (5) · Originality (15) · Distribution + co-pro evidence (18) · Credentials (15) · "FVG effect" (10) · Production facility (18) · Green protocol (2) · Audiovisual Fund contribution (2)

The big scoring weights are: **economic impact in FVG, originality, local crew, distribution evidence, production facility credentials**. A project with zero FVG anchor won't score.

## FRA fit — why this is interesting

The unlock is explicit non-EU eligibility for African production companies in three roles. Most Italian regional funds are structurally EU-only. This one isn't.

The catch is structural: the funded percentage is of *FVG-regional spend*. You don't pull €40k out of Italy for a film shot entirely in South Africa. You design in a credible FVG-anchored component — a partial shoot, post-production, scoring, animation pass, sound mix, archival research, festival-related residency — and the fund subsidises that European leg.

Where this matters for FRA's translation-layer thesis:

1. **European-leg co-production scaffolding** — for any FRA member assembling a project with a European partner, FVG is now a concrete, dated, named option to anchor that European leg.
2. **Documentary finishing** — already a PRS Phase 1 pathway (added 30 Apr 2026 from Lovinsa scoring). FVG can absorb a European finishing/post stage at 40% intensity up to €40k. Bank surety not required at ≤ €15k — meaning small finishing grants can move quickly.
3. **Animation / post** — Triggerfish-class animation post is plausible in FVG given regional facility ecosystem.
4. **Diaspora & migration narratives** — FVG geography (Italy/Slovenia/Austria/Balkans gateway) maps directly onto African–European migration narratives. The territory-promotion scoring rewards stories that surface FVG's identity.

## FRA economic note (members.md update needed if a placement closes)

This fund pays the producing entity, not FRA. If FRA represents a member on this opportunity under the MOU representation agreement:

- **Commission rate:** 10% of Placement Value per MOU v2 clause locked 22 Apr 2026.
- **Payment flow:** Default Member-collected (clause 7.3.1) — Member receives FVG grant gross, pays FRA 10% within 14 days of receipt. FRA-collected flow (7.3.2) is operationally hard here because the funder is a foreign public agency wiring to a specific production entity — pursue Member-collected.
- **Currency:** EUR → ZAR conversion, the Member's tax (Italian source income to a foreign producer has specific tax treatment — flag to Combus when this becomes real).
