Per role, work top to bottom. Stop at the first matching characterisation. Apply the resulting tax treatment.

## Step 1 — Residence

Is the worker a non-resident of South Africa for tax purposes?

- **Yes**, and the worker is an **on-camera artist or entertainer performing in SA**:
  → Apply **s47A–K**, 15% final WHT on gross. SPV withholds and remits by end of the month following payment. DTA relief only where the relevant DTA's entertainer article so provides — usually narrowly. **Stop.**
- **Yes**, and the worker is **crew (not on-camera)** present in SA:
  → Source rules (s9(2)(h)). Check DTA short-stay (typically 183 days) and dependent-personal-services / business-profits articles. If treaty exempts, document the position with the TRC and proceed without SA PAYE. If not exempt, treat under Fourth Schedule as below. **Continue to Step 2 if treaty does not exempt.**
- **No** (worker is SA resident): **Continue to Step 2.**

## Step 2 — Fourth Schedule test

Apply the independent-trader tests (paragraph 1 of the Fourth Schedule, "employee" and "independent trader"):

- Does the SPV control how, when, and where the work is done?
- Does the worker work mainly on the SPV's premises (set) at hours the SPV sets?
- Is the worker integrated into the SPV's reporting lines and call sheets?
- Does the worker bear no entrepreneurial risk (no risk of loss, no own clients)?
- Does the SPV supply the tools and equipment?

Mostly **YES** → **deemed employee**. Operate PAYE/SDL/UIF. IRP5.
Mostly **NO** → likely independent contractor; **continue to Step 3.**

## Step 3 — Personal service provider check

Is the worker invoicing through a company or trust they control?

If **yes**: check the "personal service provider" definition (Fourth Schedule, paragraph 1). If three or fewer of the PSP exclusions apply, the entity is a PSP and the SPV must withhold PAYE on payments to it at the PSP rate (effectively 27% on companies, 45% on trusts). **Stop.**

If **no** (worker invoices as a natural-person sole proprietor): treat as independent contractor. No PAYE; the worker is a provisional taxpayer responsible for their own tax. SPV does not issue an IRP5 but may need to issue an IT3(a) at year-end depending on amounts.

## Step 4 — SDL, UIF, COIDA

For any worker characterised as an **employee** in Step 2 or as a **PSP** in Step 3:
- SDL: 1% (unless annual payroll exempt threshold)
- UIF: 1% + 1% (capped)
- COIDA: per Compensation Commissioner tariff for film/TV

For true independent contractors in Step 3: none of the above.

## Step 5 — Allowances and per diems

- Subsistence allowances within the SARS-published daily rates (in-republic / out-of-republic, with/without meals) are not taxable in the worker's hands.
- Amounts above the published rate are taxable; the excess is included in remuneration if the worker is an employee.
- Travel allowances follow the deemed-expenditure tables; reimbursive travel is treated separately.

## What this template does not address

- Loan-out company structures where the loan-out is non-resident — additional layer of analysis required
- Profit participations and deferred fees — timing of PAYE accrual
- Multi-territory split shoots — apportionment of remuneration
