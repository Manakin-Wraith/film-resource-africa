## 3. South African tax treatment

### 3.1 Corporate income tax
The SPV is a South African resident company taxable on worldwide income at the prevailing CIT rate (currently 27%). Production expenditure incurred in the production of income and not of a capital nature is deductible under s11(a) of the Income Tax Act. Assessed losses carried forward are limited under s20(1)(a) to 80% of taxable income in any subsequent year.

### 3.2 VAT
The SPV will register for VAT. {Local supplies / Exported services breakdown.} Services rendered to non-resident principals not in the Republic at the time of rendering may qualify for zero-rating under s11(2)(l), subject to the documentary requirements of SARS BGR 11. This is the normal operation of the VAT Act and is **not** an incentive; investor materials should not describe it as such.

### 3.3 Withholding taxes
{Address only those flows actually present in the deal:}
- Dividends to non-resident shareholders: 20% under s64D–N, reducible by applicable DTA (typically 5–15%).
- Royalties paid by SA SPV to non-resident IP owner: 15% under s49A–D, reducible by DTA.
- Payments to non-resident artists/entertainers performing in SA: 15% final under s47A–K.

### 3.4 PAYE, SDL, UIF
The SPV will operate PAYE on employees and on freelancers who fail the Fourth Schedule independent-trader tests. The SARS *Guide on the Employers' Tax Responsibilities wrt Artists, Models or Crew in the Film Industry* governs the characterisation of on-camera talent and crew.

## 4. Incentive environment — current position

Section 12O (the historical SPCV exemption) closed to productions whose principal photography commenced on or after 1 January 2022. The dtic film and television production incentive is not accepting new applications at the date of this memo. **No financial assumption in this memo or in the financial model relies on either programme.**

3. Treaty relief on royalties / dividends / artist fees depends on holding a valid TRC for each non-resident counterparty and on the relevant DTA article surviving the PPT.
