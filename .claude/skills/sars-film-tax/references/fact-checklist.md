# Minimum Fact Set — Before Advising

Use this checklist before producing any deliverable. If a fact is missing, ask the user before assuming.

## Universal (all requests)

- Producer / production company legal name and CIPC registration number (if existing)
- SPV legal name and CIPC number (if existing) — or "to be incorporated"
- Year of assessment / financial year-end of the SPV
- Title and short logline of the production
- Format (feature, series, doc, commercial), budget bracket, principal-photography window
- Territory of principal photography (SA only / SA + co-pro / SA service)
- Counterparties paying the SPV: domicile, residence, contracting entity name
- Counterparties paid by the SPV: domicile (key for WHT)
- Existing SARS registrations: IT, VAT, PAYE/SDL/UIF — and the registration dates
- Whether the SPV has its own bank account in its own name with the production funds flowing through it

## For SPV setup / structuring requests

- Shareholder list, residence of each, percentage holding, voting vs economic split
- Whether financiers are taking equity, debt, or revenue participations
- IP ownership target: in the production SPV, in a separate HoldCo, in a foreign HoldCo
- Distribution waterfall outline (recoupment order)
- Whether any chain-of-title elements are already encumbered

## For VAT requests

- Forecast 12-month turnover and split between local supplies and exported services
- Currency of receipt for foreign principals
- Whether contracts with foreign principals exist and where the principal is contracted (entity address)
- Whether the SPV expects to be in a refund position and wants Category C monthly cycle

## For crew / talent requests

- Role descriptions of each engagement type (HOD, day-rate crew, talent, foreign talent)
- Engagement structure: PAYE, freelancer invoice, loan-out, foreign artist
- Residence and DTA position of each foreign individual
- Whether kit / equipment is supplied by the worker or the SPV
- Per diem and subsistence policy

## For cross-border requests

- TRC available for each non-resident counterparty?
- Group structure diagram (with residences and ownership %s)
- Existence and date of any inter-company licence / service agreement
- Transfer pricing documentation status
- Exchange control approvals already obtained (FinSurv)

## For collateral / investor memo requests

- Audience (SA HNW, foreign co-pro, broadcaster, public film fund)
- Length and format (one-pager, deck section, term-sheet annex)
- Whether the producer wants a "structure" memo (no numbers) or a "structure + worked example" memo (with caveated numbers)
- Confirm: no headline ROI claims, no rebate-dependent figures
