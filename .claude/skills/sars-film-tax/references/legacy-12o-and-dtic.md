# Legacy: Section 12O and the dtic Rebate — Reference (Context Only)

**Do not use this file as the basis for forward-looking advice.** Both regimes below are unavailable as planning tools for new productions at the date of this document. They are documented here to handle (a) legacy SPCVs still inside reporting windows, and (b) producer/investor questions about historical incentives.

## Section 12O — sunset

- **Scope**: Provided exemption from normal tax for receipts and accruals from the exploitation of an "approved film" derived by an SPCV, plus an exemption for dtic grants received by the SPCV.
- **Window**: Applied only where **principal photography commenced on or after 1 January 2012 and before 1 January 2022**. No new productions can enter the regime.
- **Reporting tail**: SPCVs that benefited from 12O are required to report to the NFVF on exploitation income distributed within **10 years from completion**. SPCVs from the back end of the window therefore have reporting obligations potentially until **2031–2032**.
- **Legacy advisory**: If a producer presents a 12O-approved SPCV still receiving exploitation income, focus on the reporting obligation, the distinction between exempt exploitation income and post-tail taxable income, and the treatment of distributions to investors.

If asked to model 12O on a new production: **decline**, and explain the sunset.

## dtic / NFVF Production Incentive — frozen

The Department of Trade, Industry and Competition (dtic) has historically administered a suite of production incentive programmes — SA Film and TV Production and Co-Production Incentive ("SAFP"), Foreign Film and Television Production and Post-Production Incentive, and the Black Producer-targeted programme. These provide(d) cash rebates against Qualifying South African Production Expenditure (QSAPE).

**Current status**: The incentive is **not accepting new applications** and is treated as frozen for planning purposes. Government has, at various times, indicated intent to reopen with revised terms; no such reopening should be assumed in any deliverable.

**How to talk about it**:
- Acknowledge the programme exists in principle and may return.
- Do not include any rebate percentage or QSAPE figure as a financial assumption in any model, memo, or investor deck produced under this skill.
- If a producer's plan depends on the rebate, the answer is that the production is currently un-financeable on that basis and needs a structure that works without the rebate.

## Why the SPV still matters without the incentives

The SPV/SPCV remains the right structure even without 12O or dtic, for the reasons in `spv-corporate-tax.md` (ring-fencing, IP isolation, audit attributability, financier security). The vehicle outlives the incentive.
