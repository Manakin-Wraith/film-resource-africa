Grants · Documentary

AXS Film Fund

An annual grant from AXS Lab supporting independent documentary filmmakers and nonfiction new-media creators living with disabilities, with priority for creators from historically under-represented communities. Awards up to $10,000 to 3–5 creators per year to help finish projects at any stage of production.

Deadline 31 July 2026✓ Verified 2 days ago
Cost Free to apply
Format Documentary & nonfiction new media — feature length (45+ min) or experimental, any stage
Eligibility Individual creators 18+ directing or producing their own project, not enrolled full-time in a degree programme
What you get

Grants of up to $10,000, plus mentorship from the AXS Lab network and visibility through the fund's screening partners. Funds may be applied to any production or post-production cost.

What to submit

A project statement, work sample or rough cut, production timeline, and a budget summary indicating how the grant would be applied.
