AXS Film Fund

Added by Naledi M.

An annual grant from AXS Lab supporting independent documentary filmmakers and nonfiction new-media creators living with disabilities, with priority for creators from historically under-represented communities. Awards up to $10,000 to 3–5 creators per year to help finish projects at any stage of production.

📅
Deadline 31 July 2026 ✓ Verified 2 days ago
$
CostFree to apply
🎞
FormatDocumentary & nonfiction new media — feature length (45+ min) or experimental, any stage
✓
EligibilityIndividual creators 18+ directing or producing their own project, not enrolled full-time in a degree programme

⊙ What You Get

Grants of up to $10,000, plus mentorship from the AXS Lab network and visibility through the fund's screening partners. Funds may be applied to any production or post-production cost.

▤ What to Submit

  • Project statement
  • Work sample or rough cut
  • Production timeline
  • Budget summary showing how the grant would be applied

💡 Insider Tips

Reviewers consistently favour applications where the work sample matches the tone described in the project statement. Lead with access: be specific about the story only you can tell, and show how the grant unlocks a concrete next stage rather than general support.

⏰

Applications typically open each May — set a reminder for late April to prepare materials.

Applications close 31 July 2026

Verify dates and eligibility on the official website before applying.
